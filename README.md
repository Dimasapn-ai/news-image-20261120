A program that draws one newspaper front page summing up the world news of Friday 20 November 2026: Ukraine at the centre of the Trump–Xi–Putin Shenzhen summit and its four-point peace declaration, Wall Street sliding 2.0% for a fifth straight loss even as Anthropic closed up 17% on its record Nasdaq debut, Houthi strikes on Saudi targets, Iran escalating in Hormuz, Ukraine hitting the Kerch Bridge again, COP31 down to the wire in Antalya, and the aid call for flooded Gaza — with Fiona the hippo and the Pete Rose pardon in the footer.

`main.py` is pure Python standard library: its own PNG encoder, a hand-authored stroke font, and every illustration drawn in code. It writes `entry.png` (1024x1024) deterministically — same bytes on every run.
